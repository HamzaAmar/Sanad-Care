# CONTEXT ENGINEERING: Medical Tourism Page

## Page Purpose & Goals
Create a secondary informational page (NOT homepage style) for medical tourists coming to Marrakech, Morocco. This page exists in the main navigation to:
- Educate international patients about home nursing care options in Marrakech
- Build trust with medical tourists unfamiliar with Moroccan healthcare
- Drive conversions (consultation bookings, contact form fills, phone calls)
- Rank for medical tourism + Marrakech nursing keywords

## Target Audience Profile
PRIMARY: International patients (30-75 years old) traveling to Marrakech for:
- Surgical procedures (cosmetic, orthopedic, dental, fertility)
- Post-operative recovery
- Chronic illness management
- Elderly care needs

SECONDARY: Family members and medical tourism facilitators

Geographic focus: International patients → Marrakech, Morocco
Language: English (nurses speak Arabic, French, English, Spanish)

## Content Structure & Storytelling Flow

### Section 1: Hero/Introduction
**Tone:** Reassuring & medical
**Goal:** Immediately address the pain point (recovering abroad is stressful)
**Key message:** "You came to Marrakech for treatment. We'll make sure your recovery is safe, comfortable, and stress-free."
**CTA:** Primary contact option

### Section 2: Why Home Nursing Care for Medical Tourists?
**Tone:** Educational & empathetic
**Goal:** Explain the benefits vs. hospital/hotel-only recovery
**Address pain points:**
- Language barriers with hospital staff
- No post-discharge follow-up
- Uncomfortable hospital environments
- Family anxiety about care quality abroad
**Include:** Brief mention of "See our Why Us page for our full story"

### Section 3: Our Medical Tourism Services
**Tone:** Highly professional & clinical
**Goal:** List care types with medical credibility
**Services to highlight:**
- Post-surgery nursing care (wound care, drains, catheters)
- Medication administration & IV therapy
- Injections & specialized treatments
- Physiotherapy at home
- 24/7 nursing assistance
- Chronic disease management
- Elderly care
- Palliative care
- Medical equipment provision (oxygen, hospital beds, mobility aids)

**Care duration options:** 1 hour, daily visits, weekly, monthly, or subscription-based

### Section 4: Where We Provide Care
**Tone:** Luxury & comfort
**Goal:** Show flexibility and convenience
**Locations:** Hotels, Riads, Private villas, Airbnb rentals, Patient homes
**Bonus:** We coordinate with your accommodation

### Section 5: How It Works (Patient Journey)
**Tone:** Simple & stress-free
**Goal:** Remove friction and confusion
**Steps:**
1. Contact us before or after arriving in Marrakech
2. We assess your needs (phone/video/in-person)
3. We assign licensed nurses (multilingual, experienced)
4. Care begins same-day or scheduled
5. Coordination with your doctors/hospital
6. Ambulance services available if needed (pickup from airport/hotel/home)

- I prefer this section to be as timeline, I think it will be more clear to the user.

### Section 6: Why Medical Tourists Trust Us
**Tone:** Trust-building & authoritative
**Goal:** Overcome "unreliable caregiver" fear
**Trust signals:**
- Licensed and trained nurses
- Doctor-coordinated care plans
- Experience with international patients (multiple nationalities)
- Multilingual staff (English, French, Arabic, Spanish)
- 24/7 support line
- Same-day service availability
- Hospital partnerships in Marrakech
- Insurance and embassy coordination support

### Section 7: Pricing & Transparency
**Tone:** Professional & honest
**Goal:** Set expectations without sticker shock
**Message:**
- Pricing varies based on care type, duration, and complexity
- Each service includes core nursing care + specific treatments
- Additional services added as needed (transparent pricing)
- Packages available for post-surgery recovery (e.g., 7-day, 14-day)
- Contact for personalized quote

### Section 8: Emotional Close + CTA
**Tone:** Human & emotional
**Goal:** Final conversion push with empathy
**Message:** "We know recovering far from home can feel isolating. Our nurses don't just provide medical care—they provide comfort, language support, and peace of mind. We do this because we love helping people heal."
**CTA:** Book consultation / WhatsApp / Call now

## Writing Style Guidelines
- **NOT a homepage:** No hero slogans, no "Welcome to..." — dive straight into value
- **Medical credibility:** Use clinical terminology appropriately (but explain jargon)
- **Storytelling:** Each section transitions logically (problem → solution → trust → action)
- **Avoid generic AI phrases:** No "embark on a journey," "our team of dedicated professionals," "we pride ourselves"
- **Active voice:** "We coordinate with your doctor" not "Coordination with doctors is provided"
- **Specific, not vague:** "Licensed Moroccan nurses fluent in English" not "qualified healthcare professionals"
- **Emotional intelligence:** Acknowledge fear/anxiety without being patronizing

## Conversion Elements
- Minimum 3 CTAs throughout page
- Phone number, WhatsApp, contact form options
- Internal link to "Why Us" page (mentioned in Section 2)
- Urgency where appropriate ("Same-day service available")

## Content Depth Rules
- Total page length: 1,200-1,800 words
- Each section: 150-250 words
- Paragraph length: 2-4 sentences max
- Use subheadings liberally for scannability
- Include 1-2 patient-centric examples/scenarios (e.g., "Sarah from the UK had knee surgery...")

## Technical Notes
- Write for non-technical international audience
- Assume reader knows nothing about Moroccan healthcare system
- Address unstated concerns (safety, reliability, cost, communication)
- Subtle cultural sensitivity (Morocco as modern, safe, high-quality destination)

## What to AVOID
- ❌ Generic stock phrases
- ❌ Homepage-style hero sections
- ❌ Over-selling or hype language
- ❌ Walls of text without breaks
- ❌ Medical jargon without context
- ❌ Ignoring pain points (language, unfamiliarity, anxiety)
